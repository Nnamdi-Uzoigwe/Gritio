Privacy policy

Gritio. Last updated: REPLACE_WITH_RELEASE_DATE

In short: Gritio keeps your goals, habits, tasks and reflections on your phone. It has no accounts, it does not send your information anywhere, and it has no advertising or tracking.

What Gritio stores on your phone

Your name if you choose to give one, your goals with their milestones and steps, your habits and their history, your tasks, your reflections, weekly reviews and personal rules, and your preferences. All of it is stored in a database on your phone.

What Gritio does not collect

The developer of Gritio does not receive any of your information. Gritio has no accounts and no servers of its own. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or any service that collects data about you, and it does not sell or share your information. The app works fully without an internet connection.

The only time Gritio opens a web page is when you tap the privacy policy link in the app. That page opens in your phone's browser.

Permissions, and why

  • Notifications: only if you switch on reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your phone and are not sent from any server.
  • Biometrics or passcode: only if you switch on App Lock. Your fingerprint or face data never reaches Gritio. Your phone only tells Gritio whether you unlocked successfully.
  • Files: only when you create or restore a backup, through your phone's own share and file screens.

Backups you create

A backup file is a plain copy of your data. It is not encrypted, and it is yours to keep safe. Gritio never uploads it. Where you save it, and who can see it there, is your choice.

Your phone's own backups

Your phone's backup features, such as iCloud or Google backup, may keep a copy of app data under your own account, depending on your phone's settings. Gritio does not control this.

Deleting your data

You can edit or delete anything you create in Gritio at any time. Removing Gritio from your phone removes the data it stored on the phone itself.

Children

Gritio is a general purpose planning app and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal information, there is no child data to collect or delete.

Changes to this policy

If Gritio ever changes how it treats information, this page will be updated before the change reaches the app, and the date above will change.

Contact
