Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Gritio has no accounts and no servers, and it works fully offline.

How do I back up or move my data?

Open Settings, then Data and backup. Create a backup file and save it where you like, for example your cloud drive or email. To restore, open the same screen on the new phone and choose the backup file. A restore replaces what is on the phone at that moment. A backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

My reminders arrive late or not at all

First check Settings, then Reminders in Gritio, and make sure notifications are allowed in your phone settings. On some Android phones, battery saving features delay or stop reminders for apps you do not open often. In your phone's battery settings, set Gritio to unrestricted or not optimised. On iPhone, check that no Focus mode is silencing Gritio.

I turned on App Lock and cannot get in

App Lock uses your phone's own biometrics or passcode, so unlocking Gritio works the same as unlocking your phone. If your phone no longer has a screen lock, Gritio turns App Lock off by itself.

How do I delete everything?

Remove Gritio from your phone and the data it stored goes with it. You can also delete individual goals, habits, tasks and reflections inside the app. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may keep a copy under your own account, and you control that in your phone settings.

Why is there a limit of 10 active goals and habits?

Gritio is built around a small number of things done well. Archived and completed goals and archived habits do not count towards the limit.
