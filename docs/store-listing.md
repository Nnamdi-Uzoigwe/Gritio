## Full description

Gritio is a calm, private place to plan your day, build habits and work toward the goals that matter to you.

PLAN YOUR DAY
Add tasks, schedule steps from your goals and see everything you committed to on one Today screen. Move what you did not get to, without guilt.

BUILD HABITS THAT LAST
Choose the days a habit happens, set a smaller minimum version for hard days, and watch your consistency over time. A day off never erases what you have already done.

REACH REAL GOALS
Break a goal into milestones and steps, schedule the next step and see your progress as honest numbers. Start from a template or from scratch.

LOOK BACK AND ADJUST
Write a short daily reflection, complete a weekly review and keep a few personal rules where you will see them.

REMINDERS WHEN YOU WANT THEM
Optional reminders for tasks, habits and steps, plus an optional morning plan and evening reflection. Everything is scheduled on your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your goals, habits and reflections stay on your phone, and Gritio works fully offline. Create a backup file whenever you like and restore it when you need to. An optional App Lock uses your phone's biometrics or passcode.

MADE TO BE CALM
Light and dark themes, large text support, screen reader labels and gentle celebrations you can switch off.

Gritio is built for steady progress, one honest day at a time.

## Apple review notes

Gritio has no account or login, and everything is stored on the device. Notifications and App Lock (Face ID or passcode) are optional and only used if turned on. To explore, create a goal or a habit from the Goals and Habits tabs. The app makes no network requests.
